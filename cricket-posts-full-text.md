"-Cricket World Cup T20 in Calcutta"
"-Rugby Six Nations game for Calcutta Cup in Edinburgh "
""
"Yes, in a strange and delightful coincidence we have the national teams battle it out in cricket and rugby today one game in Calcutta and the other for the Calcutta Cup "
""
"Of course since the years the Calcutta Cup rivalry started in 1879 the city’s name has changed to Kolkata "
""
"So, What is the story ? "
""
"The Calcutta Rugby and Football Club, was founded in 1873 in what is now Kolkata, India. "
""
"The club was formed by British expats and soldiers who held a popular Christmas Day rugby match between teams representing England and Scotland. However, the club disbanded in 1878 because rugby was losing popularity compared to sports like polo and tennis. "
""
"To create a lasting memorial, the members withdrew their remaining funds all of 270 silver rupees and had them melted down to create the trophy. They gifted it to the Rugby Football Union (RFU) in England with the request that it be played for annually … and it has been !"
""
