Time Out

A white paper by Tiger Tyagarajan

The pessimist's case on AI has become precise. It has timelines — a one-in-three chance of superintelligence within a year. It has mechanisms — systems that learn to game their own scores, labs that can be breached in hours. And it has a prescription: pause, before it is too late.

This paper is the optimist looking at the pessimist's view of the world. It takes the case at full strength — and then answers it on the evidence. Capability is not deployment. Claims that cannot be checked earn small updates, not doubled timelines. And when answers get cheap, judgment is what gets scarce. It ends with the only question that matters: what would make us stop?
