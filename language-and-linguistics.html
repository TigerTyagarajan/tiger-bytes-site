Language and Linguistics

Words are where my curiosity goes to play — where they come from, how they travel between cultures, and what they pick up on the way. I am learning European Portuguese in Lisbon, slowly and with great pleasure, and I collect these as I go.

Arigato, Obrigado

Have you ever wondered where a word you use every day actually came from? In my curiosity-driven life I dive deep into etymology — the study of the history of words, tracing their origins, their development, and how their meanings change over time.

Living in Lisbon and reading Portuguese history, I stumbled on this one: "arigato", the Japanese word for thank you, was introduced into Japanese from the Portuguese "obrigado". Traders carried the word across the world centuries ago, and it never left. One word, two hemispheres, a whole history of contact between cultures — exactly the kind of rabbit hole I cannot resist.

First posted on LinkedIn, October 2025.

The Oldest Bookshop in the World

Spending three days in Lisbon, a city and country I have come to really like — I do fall for anything new that I can dive deep into and learn with the passion and curiosity of a child.

I am learning Portuguese on Duolingo. It's very cool — it gets my learning juices going. And in Lisbon I wandered into a bookstore — for those who know me, no surprise — but not any bookstore: Bertrand, the oldest operating bookshop in the world. I bought two books, and they stamped them with the "oldest bookstore in the world" stamp. Very cool. Learning a language where it is spoken, surrounded by its history — there is no better classroom.

First posted on LinkedIn, January 2025.

Fifty Languages, One Film

It's always great to grab a coffee and shoot the breeze with someone like Shibu Nambiar — I come away from every interaction having learnt something. This time, for example: how AI is being used to film a movie in one language and make it available in fifty, with full facial-expression and lip-movement changes to match the audio. When curiosity is shared, it's a whole new thing.
